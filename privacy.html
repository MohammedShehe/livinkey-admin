Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Information we store

The Livinkey admin console stores resident information such as name, contact details, nationality, identity and immigration documents (e.g. passport, visa, FRRO, C-Form, arrival stamp), room assignment and payment history, solely to operate PG accommodation services.

2. How admin data is used

Admin login details and one-time verification codes are used only to authenticate access to the console and are never shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Document storage

Uploaded identity and travel documents are visible only to admins with granted access and can be downloaded or deleted directly from a member's profile by an authorized admin.

4. Messaging data

Conversations between admins and members/guests are automatically removed 30 days after being sent, and can also be deleted earlier by an admin.

5. Your rights

Residents may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data by contacting their property admin or livinkey@gmail.com.

6. Security

Access to resident data is restricted through role-based permissions and two-factor (OTP) verification for every admin sign-in.
